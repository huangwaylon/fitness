# 0009 — No sleep tracking: four fruits
**Status:** Accepted (round 4; user input). Amends [0002](0002-one-scoring-model.md). Step days are now 4 of 7 and Peach also counts step-derived minutes: [0017](0017-round-4-rule-changes-and-rule-versions.md).

**Context.** Neither partner wears the Watch to bed, so sleep can't be measured without manual work.

## Decision
| Fruit | Category | Pts |
|---|---|---|
| 🍑 Peach | Active minutes vs. weekly target (30) + ≥ 1 rest day (5) | 35 |
| 🥥 Coconut | Strength days (PT session, Strength/Core/Pilates ≥ 15 min, confirmed gym visit) | 25 |
| 🍎 Apple | Step goal on 5 of 7 days (Tokyo tsuyu Jun 1–Jul 20: or ≥ 30 active min) | 15 |
| 🍐 Pear | 3 personal eating habits, 15 of 21 ticks | 25 |

- **Sleep:** an optional, unscored iPhone Sleep Focus bedtime reminder. Not shown on the main screens.
- **Recovery hints** use daytime resting HR, walking HR average and recent training load (no overnight HRV). They're still suggestions only.
- Four fruits per tree also reduces visual clutter.
