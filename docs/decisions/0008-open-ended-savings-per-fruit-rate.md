# 0008 — Open-ended savings: 1 fruit = ¥50
**Status:** **Superseded by [0019](0019-bells-daily-earning-two-pots.md)** (ベル per day instead of ¥50 per fruit, 2026-10-30). Accepted round 4 (user input). Supersedes the 85-threshold, shortfall bank and fixed-goal parts of 0003 and 0006.

**User input:** "No particular destination, maybe just saving up as much as possible… use [¥520,000] as an estimate to scale the amount earned with each action."

## Decision
- **Personal:** each fruit earns **¥50** into the Trip Jar. Max 100 fruit = **¥5,000/person/week**.
- **Team bonus:** **¥20 × the lower partner's score** (a paused partner counts as 100). Max **¥2,000/week**, about 17% of the maximum.
- **Treat Purse:** unchanged, a flat **¥1,000/person/week at ≥ 60 fruit** (a paused week counts).
- **Dropped:** "85 = full share", the shortfall bank (`recover:` ledger entries), the fixed target and the single destination. Overtraining stays unpaid because every category caps at the person's own target and active minutes have a daily cap.
- **No up-front pre-commitment.** A **payday Transfer Day** reminder (default the 25th, 給料日) moves last month's earned yen to a savings account, optionally via a standing bank auto-transfer of the expected amount (≈ ¥45,000/month) adjusted monthly.
- **Jar presentation:** open-ended, with a **"what this could buy" ladder**. The Jar shows **only the next rung** (goal-gradient), and reaching a rung is celebrated at Sunday harvest.
  Sample rungs (editable): ¥30,000 温泉日帰り+ごちそう · ¥80,000 箱根 onsen weekend · ¥150,000 沖縄 · ¥300,000 台湾/ソウル · ¥500,000 ハワイ · ¥700,000+ ヨーロッパ.
