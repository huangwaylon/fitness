# Round 1 Review: Motivational Coach

Lens: whether the game loops are clear, how the rewards are framed, whether engagement holds over 12 months, and couple dynamics. I start by criticizing my own memo.

**Self-critique first.** My memo invented too much: Miles, Bells, Island Locker, Lost & Found, Rainy Day, Stretch Fund, Cozy Days, Comeback Quests, Snowflakes. That is too many nouns for two people to keep in their heads. It also uses Nintendo names (Bells, Nook Miles, Isabelle, Blathers, Nook's Cranny), which break the designer's IP guardrails (§12). I withdraw them in favor of fruit, the jar, tickets, and Juniper. My daily 15-Mile card and my Move-ring slot also conflict with the fitness coach's better weekly-minutes, no-calories model. I adopt the coach's model and drop the Move ring from scoring.

## 1. What I endorse
- **Fitness coach:** weekly targets measured against each person's own baseline. A hard ceiling (more is not better). Adaptive rules (advance, hold, step back, never auto-raise). Rest earns points. Sick, gentle, and pause modes. Joint-workout detection. Equal points for unequal bodies. No money for calories or VO2 max.
- **Nutritionist:** "Treat Meal, never cheat meal." Treats never cost points. A 3-habit starter set, with one habit added per quarter and a cap of 5. Water read automatically from Health. Partner co-logging. Weight never shown and never paid.
- **Designer:** Juniper's tone. The voyage map whose milestones are *real trip actions* ("Flights booked"), which is excellent for vivid goals. Both partners tapping "Drop it in the jar." Missed weeks still get a ceremony ("The tide comes back in"). Logging a treat counts as a win, so honesty is rewarded. Each partner can hide individual metrics.
- **Engineer:** an append-only ledger that only ever adds money (no clawbacks). From a motivation standpoint this is exactly right: a reward never gets taken back. The monthly "Transfer Day, I moved it" ritual is the commitment device, and it costs nothing to build.
- **Lead:** the success criteria. "Still opening weekly in month 12" is the right north star.

## 2. Conflicts and concerns, with fixes
| # | Concern | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| a | Designer's **"perfect day" (all 4 fruits) = +bonus $** is a daily all-or-nothing reward. It invites the what-the-hell effect once one fruit is missed, and the fitness coach warns against exactly this ("closing all rings"). | A perfect day earns a golden leaf (cosmetic) and **no money**. Money comes only from weekly points. |
| b | Daily fruit for **exercise minutes** doesn't match a **weekly** minutes target. | The peach ripens in stages through the week (¼, ½, ¾, full) toward the weekly target. Steps, sleep, and eats stay as daily fruit counted out of 5 per week. |
| c | **Island upgrades gated on "N good weeks in a row"** freeze the island visibly during a rough patch, which is a quiet punishment. | Upgrades unlock on **cumulative fruit** (they always progress, just slower). Streaks only earn shells in the museum. |
| d | **Partner-facing nudge** ("Bean is 2,900 steps from a perfect day!") shown to the *other* partner drifts toward monitoring and nagging (Lewis & Rook). | Partner-facing prompts are only invitations to do something *together* ("Evening stroll together?"), at most 1 a day. The person being described can turn these off. |
| e | **Notification creep:** designer meal-time pushes (up to 4), plus the engineer's evening reminder, goal-hit celebrations, waves, the ceremony, and Transfer Day. | A **single notification budget of 2 per day per person**, plus Sunday and Transfer Day. Meal-time pushes go away (see C3). |
| f | **Bonus sprawl:** the nutritionist's cooked-together, meal-prep, and new-recipe bonuses, plus the coach's joint, team-week, monthly quest, focus quest, and season finale. Each is fine on its own, but together they're noise and break the calibration. | Two couple rewards stay permanent: joint workouts and the team share. Everything else becomes a **rotating seasonal quest** (one active at a time), which also solves novelty. |
| g | The coach's example ($10 per 100 pts → ~$2,000) doesn't reach the $3,000 goal. | Calibrate with C2 below: 80 pts = full weekly share. |
| h | Season names start at "Spring Sprout," but the start date is about Nov 2026. | Name seasons by start date: S1 = Cozy Winter, S2 = Blossom, S3 = Sunny, S4 = Harvest, then the voyage. |
| i | My memo's names (above). | Use the designer's vocabulary throughout. |

## 3. Gaps (nobody owns these yet)
1. **Content cadence for 12 months.** Engagement usually drops after the first few weeks or months. We need a calendar: one seasonal quest/event per month, one new island upgrade a week, a new villager or visitor each season, and about 30% of cosmetics held back for months 7–12. Proposed owner: motivational coach plus designer, written into the proposal.
2. **Year-end leftovers and life after year 1.** These need one sentence in onboarding (see C2) and a "Year 2 / maintenance mode" decision point at the vacation.
3. **When one partner lags.** If adherence differs by more than 20 points for 4+ weeks, show the lagging partner a *private* "make my targets cozier?" suggestion. Never show the gap to the other partner.
4. **An onboarding "island charter."** Both partners set the rules together, and any rule change needs both to approve (the designer has the approval flow). Rules they chose themselves feel less controlling (self-determination theory).
5. **An escape hatch for eating worries.** "If tracking stops feeling good" pauses eating scores and hides numbers. This needs a home in the UI.
6. **Fading the money.** Season 1 can lead with dollars. From S2 on, the Island tab leads with fruit and the island, and dollars appear on the Fund tab and in the ceremony.

## 4. Positions on lead conflict points
**C1 (one scoring model).** One **100-point weekly card per person** with 5 categories, mapping 1:1 to 5 fruits (C4). It uses the coach's rules with re-weighted points:

| Fruit | Category | Pts | Rule (coach/nutritionist definitions) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🍑 Peach | Exercise minutes vs. weekly target (daily cap applies) | 30 | 0 below 50%, linear from 50% to 100% |
| 🥥 Coconut | Strength days vs. target (1–3) | 20 | split across target days; a 3rd day never pays more |
| 🍎 Apple | Daily movement: steps-goal days | 10 | 2 pts/day, max 5 days |
| 🫐 Plum | Rest: sleep nights (2/night, max 5 = 10) + 1 recovery day (5) | 15 | |
| 🍐 Pear | Eats: 3 habits, days met | 25 | about 25%, at the low end of the nutritionist's range |

Stand hours and active-days become badges only. Joint workouts are a separate couple line (C2).

**C2 (simpler economy).** Cut it to **three things: Fruit (points), the Jar (real $), and Tickets (treats).** One sentence: *"Healthy habits grow fruit, and every Sunday your fruit fills the trip jar and earns treat tickets."* The rules behind that sentence:
- Weekly pot = $3,000 / 52 = $57.69. Each person has a **$26 share**, filled by points: **80 points fills it** (≈ $0.325/pt). A **$5.70 team share** pays at the *lower* partner's fill level, and a paused partner counts as full.
- **Catching up, without a Lost & Found:** points from 80 to 100 pay extra **only while the jar is behind the pace line**, so a strong week recovers a weak one. Effort is still capped at 100, so there's no incentive to overtrain. At about 85% adherence you reach the full $3,000.
- **What happens to unearned money:** it stays in the bank and isn't spent on fun. That's stated once at setup and never shown as a counter.
- Gone: Miles (cosmetics unlock from cumulative fruit, so there's no second currency), Locker (it's just the bank account), Lost & Found, Rainy Day, Stretch, and named token types. One kind of "rest day" covers sick, gentle, and pause (the coach's mechanics, one name).

**C3 (eating check-in).** **One evening card with 3 habit toggles** (nutritionist starter set), about 5 seconds, partner co-logging, 48-hour backfill, plus a neutral "🎟️ treat meal" chip that counts as checking in. Drop the per-meal Healthy/Balanced/Treat rating. Four a day breaks the 10-second budget, and rating meals is moralizing.

**C4 (fruit metaphor).** Add a **coconut for strength** ("strong as a coconut"), giving the 5 fruits/categories in C1. Rest goes into the plum.

**C5 (settlement timing).** **Provisional ceremony on Sunday at 7:30 pm.** The ritual and the Monday fresh start matter more than precision. Late Health data arrives as "late fruit" top-ups shown at the start of the *next* ceremony. The no-clawback ledger makes this safe.

**C6 (treat money source).** **A separate treat budget.** Tickets come from the same fruit: each person reaching 80 points in a week earns ½ ticket toward a dinner out (≈ $50 a month). The trip jar is never raided.

**C7 (partner visibility).** Default to **fruit/no fruit and % of own goal**. Raw numbers are opt-in per metric. Calories are never shown side by side. Sleep and eats are fruit-only by default.

**C8 (daily dollars).** **Daily = fruit only.** The Fund tab may show "this week: $18 of $26 filled" as a progress bar. Remove the "+$3 today" chip: daily dollars aren't well-defined under weekly scoring, and they make the app feel like wages (controlling feedback). Immediate rewards stay strong through fruit growing, sound, and Juniper.
