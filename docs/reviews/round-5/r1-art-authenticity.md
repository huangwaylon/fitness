# Round 5 · R1 · NH art authenticity (v4)

Reviewer: senior game artist (ACNH). Date: 2026-10-29, 12:26 JST. Live app `127.0.0.1:5173/fitness/?demo`, the season/theme variants, and `/dev/characters`, at 393, 375 and 320 px. I watched 32 s of idle life and ran R2/R5 plus the tree and villager taps. Screenshots are in `.shots/r5/r1-*`.

**Verdict.** The composition reads as NH at a glance: a Resident Services backdrop, seasonal hedges, the lattice grass, official villagers and しずえ, and correct October flowers (tea olive, cosmos, mums). Most of what still feels off comes from **which** official art is used **when**, not from missing art.
