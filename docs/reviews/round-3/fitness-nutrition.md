# Round 3 review: fitness coach + nutritionist (implemented scoring)

**Scope:** `app/src/lib/config/rules.ts`, `app/src/lib/scoring/*`, the habit strings in i18n, and the check-in/cozy/reminder code paths. I checked them against PROPOSAL §2–3 and ADRs 0002/0008/0009/0011. The 69 scoring tests pass. I probed `scoreWeek` with throwaway scripts.

**What matches the design:** the daily cap is `min(90, max(60, ½ target))` (A 67.5, B 60). Strength sources and the ≥ 15 min / ≥ 30 min visit thresholds match. Step days, including the tsuyu Jun 1–Jul 20 substitute at ≥ 30 min, match. Pear is off Dec 29–Jan 3, and easy weeks use ×0.7 on Peach/Coconut/Apple. Cozy prorating uses exact fractions. Calibration pays a flat 60 at ≥ 6 data days. Season 1 targets and habit keys match nutritionist-personalized. Overtraining earns nothing extra, since every category caps at target. No copy frames food as good or bad.

| ID | Sev | What | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Blocking** | **A week of doing nothing can pay 100.** Cozy Days are capped only per calendar month, so a week that spans two months can hold up to 7 (e.g. Mar 29–Apr 4 2027: 3 + 4). With 0 eligible days, every category pays in full: probe gave score **100** with no activity (¥5,000 + Purse + team). With 6 cozy days, 20 min of activity and one 15-min core session gave 85. | `actions.ts:66-71` (month cap only); `score.ts:18`, `:47`; test `score.test.ts:256` locks it in | Add a per-week cap (≤ 3 Cozy Days per Mon–Sun) on top of 4/month. Longer illness becomes a pause (v1). Until pause ships, cap a week with ≥ 4 cozy days at 60. |
| F2 | Should | **The rest-day bonus is basically free, and 7/7 training still scores 100.** "Hard" requires avg HR ≥ 77% of HRmax (A ≈ 147, B ≈ 149 bpm). Strength sessions average 110–135, so they never qualify. Probes: 7 × 75-min strength + 90 min/day scored **100, rest = 7**. 7 × 60-min runs at 146 bpm also gave rest = 7. This breaks the round-4 aim that 100 is impossible without a rest day. The ≥ 45-min clause from round 4 was dropped. | `rules.ts:37`; `score.ts:64-71` | A rest day = no strength day **and** no workout ≥ 45 min **and** none ≥ 20 min at ≥ 77%. A Cozy Day still counts. |
| F3 | Should | **Backdated Cozy Days on days you trained pay double.** You can set a cozy day up to 2 days back, until Wednesday's lock, and that day's activity still counts. **Design soundness:** S1 targets suit both people: B at 85 min, 6,250 steps, 2 strength days set by her trainer; A at 135 min, 7,500 steps, 2× A/B. B's under-fuelling safeguard is in place: `eat_lunch` is habit 1, and nothing pays more for training more. No weight or calorie data is used anywhere.
